Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This is a starter policy. It must be checked against the actual data collected, stored, logged, and processed by Knull and its hosting and AI providers before publication.

1. Scope

This policy describes privacy considerations for the Knull Discord application and its related services. Discord processes information independently under its own privacy policy.

2. Information That May Be Processed

Depending on the enabled features, Knull may process Discord user identifiers, server and channel identifiers, message or interaction content submitted to the application, command inputs, and technical logs needed to operate and troubleshoot the service. The actual categories depend on the implementation.

3. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used to respond to commands, provide requested features, maintain security, diagnose errors, and prevent abuse. Information should only be collected and retained when necessary for the application's legitimate operational purposes.

4. Storage and Third-Party Providers

Depending on the configuration, information may be processed by hosting providers, infrastructure services, or AI model providers. Before publishing this policy, the operator should identify the providers actually used and explain relevant data transfers, storage practices, and retention periods.

5. Retention and Security

Retention depends on the data and features enabled. The operator should establish appropriate retention periods, delete information when it is no longer needed, protect credentials such as bot tokens, and apply suitable access controls.

6. Your Choices and Requests

For questions or requests relating to information handled directly by Knull, contact the operator using the details below. Requests concerning information held by Discord should be directed to Discord.

7. Children and Applicable Law

Knull does not override Discord's minimum age requirements. The operator should review applicable privacy and data-protection laws for the locations and users involved.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when Knull's features or data practices change. The last-updated date will be revised when changes are made.
